Why nota
Built different.

One design language

Every app shares the same visual DNA — Syne, DM Sans, dark-first design, smooth animations. Moving between them feels natural, not jarring.

Privacy by default

Your data lives in your account. No ads, no data selling, no analytics tracking you around the web. What you write stays yours.

Fast by design

No bloat, no loading screens, no unnecessary features. Every interaction is instant. Optimized to stay out of your way.

Multilingual

All apps support English, Arabic, French, and Russian — with full RTL support for Arabic. Built for the world from day one.

Light & dark mode

Every app ships with a beautiful light and dark theme, carefully crafted for each color palette. Your eyes, your choice.

Cloud-synced

Powered by Supabase, everything syncs in real time across all your devices. Start on your phone, continue on your laptop.
